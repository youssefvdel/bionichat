# bionichat — logo brief

## What it is
bionichat is a desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) that turns web AIs —
Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude — into agents that can act on your own PC.
The app runs a local MCP hub on your machine, exposes your files, shell,
and tools to the AI chat, and tunnels out to the public internet so a
browser chat can reach your computer. You watch and approve everything
from the dashboard.

One line: **chat with arms and legs — your AI chat, given hands on your PC.**

## The name
- **bionic** = augmented limbs, grafted power. The chat grows arms and legs.
- **chat** = it lives inside a conversation (Gemini / ChatGPT / Claude).
- Pronounced "bie-ON-ik-chat". Lowercase spelling: **bionichat**.

## Who it's for
Technical users and developers who live in terminals and AI chats.
People who run local servers, tunnels, and CLIs — they respect sharp,
honest, technical design and hate corporate decoration.

## Brand adjectives
Sharp · alive · technical · trustworthy · direct. A tool, not a toy.
No playfulness, no gradients, no mascots, no emoji energy.

## What the logo must do
1. App icon in the taskbar/dock and window title bar (16–24 px, must read tiny).
2. Custom title-bar mark next to the name, drawn in one colour.
3. Dashboard header on a near-black terminal UI.
4. Later: favicon, installer icon, README header.

## Visual world it lives in
- Dark terminal dashboard: ground `#171719`, cards `#131217`, ink white.
- One accent: electric blue `#4a8ad6` (only selection marks and the primary action).
- Figures set in monospace with tabular digits; text in a neutral sans.
- Flat shapes only. Hairline 1px borders, 6px corners, pill buttons.
- The product's own words: hub, tunnel, tools, approvals, activity.

## Hard constraints
- One colour. Must work in solid black on white AND solid white on black.
- No gradients, no shadows, no fine detail that dies under ~48 px.
- No text inside the finished mark (the wordmark is set separately).
- Timeless over trendy. An identifier, not an explanation.

## Clichés to avoid
Generic chat bubbles, plugs, shields, upward arrows, robot heads,
hexagon-nodes with dots (overused in dev tools), terminal `>_`
on its own, gears, clouds.

## Directions we already like (riff on these or beat them)
- **A — "hub-spur"**: a hexagonal boundary ring pierced by three spokes
  that meet in a hub dot. Story: outside tools reaching through the
  boundary into your PC. Geometric, bold, fills its frame.
- **B — "power-b"**: a lowercase letter **b** whose bowl is an electric
  power ring, the stem grafted into it. Story: chat with power.
  Reads best at tiny sizes and says the name.

## Deliver
3 distinct black-on-white concepts as SVG (viewBox 0 0 256 256),
each with a one-sentence idea. No colour yet.
